# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Give constructive feedback, welcome different
experience levels, and focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, or discussions.

Report concerns privately through the contact options at https://hakkam.com.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or
restrict participation proportionately. Reports will be handled with discretion.
Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.
